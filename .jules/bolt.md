## 2025-02-21 - Remove Artificial Delays and Convert to Async in Identity PublicApi Endpoints
**Learning:** Multiple PublicApi endpoints (such as UserListEndpoint and RoleListEndpoint) contained artificial 1-second delay `Task.Delay(1000)` calls that added unnecessary request latency. Additionally, synchronous queries like `.ToList()` blocking the thread pool can be optimized to asynchronous `ToListAsync(ct)` calls using Microsoft.EntityFrameworkCore extensions. This reduces PublicApi integration test suite execution time by approximately 12 seconds (from ~17s to ~5s) and dramatically improves the throughput under load.
**Action:** Always scan PublicApi and Core endpoints for artificial latency/blocking calls and proactively convert synchronous Database/Identity queries to their asynchronous counterparts to maximize request-processing efficiency.
